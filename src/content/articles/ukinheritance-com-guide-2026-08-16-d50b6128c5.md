## Before You Apply

Before applying for probate, you must confirm that you’re eligible to apply.

You must also find out if you need to pay Inheritance Tax.

## After You Apply

Your application will be reviewed by the Probate Service.

If you ordered copies of your probate document for use outside the UK, these will take longer to arrive than your UK copy.

## Staying in the UK

If your right to live in the UK depends on your relationship with someone who died, you might need to apply for a new visa. This applies if your partner who died served as a member of HM Forces.

## FAQ

### What must I do before applying for probate?

You must confirm that you’re eligible to apply and find out if you need to pay Inheritance Tax.

### How is my probate application handled?

Your application will be reviewed by the Probate Service.

### Will my overseas probate copies take longer?

Yes, copies for use outside the UK take longer to arrive than your UK copy.

### What if my UK residency depends on the deceased?

If your right to live in the UK depends on your relationship with the deceased, you might need to apply for a new visa, including if the deceased served as a member of HM Forces.
