## Overview

Inheritance Tax is a tax on the estate (the property, money and possessions) of someone who’s died. Funds from your estate are used to pay Inheritance Tax to HM Revenue and Customs (HMRC).

## Gifts and Inheritance Tax

People you give gifts to might have to pay Inheritance Tax, but only if you give away more than £325,000 and die within 7 years.
