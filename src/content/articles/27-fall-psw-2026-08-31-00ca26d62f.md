## UK PSW Tier Reform: What the Changes Mean for Arts Graduates

The UK's Post-Study Work (PSW) visa is undergoing a tiered reform that will affect the duration of stay for graduates in different disciplines. Under the planned changes, arts graduates will see a shorter period of post-study work permission compared to graduates in other fields. This reform is part of a broader effort to align post-study work rights with the UK's economic priorities.

## How the Tier System Works

The new tier system categorises graduates based on their subject of study. While the exact tiers have not been fully detailed, it is clear that arts and humanities graduates will be placed in a lower tier, resulting in a reduced stay duration. This contrasts with graduates in STEM or other priority subjects, who may retain longer post-study work rights.

## Master's Application Pitfalls to Avoid

For prospective master's students, particularly those in arts disciplines, the reform introduces several pitfalls in the application process:

- **Course Selection**: Choosing a course that falls into a lower tier could limit post-study work opportunities. Applicants should research how their intended programme is classified under the new system.
- **Timing**: The reform may affect the application timeline for the 27Fall intake. Ensure you are aware of any changes to visa requirements and deadlines.
- **Documentation**: Prepare all necessary documents, including proof of funds and English language proficiency, as these remain critical for visa approval.
- **Career Planning**: With a shorter stay, arts graduates need to plan their job search and career progression more strategically from the outset.
