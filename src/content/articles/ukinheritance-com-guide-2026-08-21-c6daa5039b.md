## Before You Apply

Before applying for probate, you must check that you are eligible to apply. You must also find out if you need to pay Inheritance Tax.

## After You Apply

Your application will be reviewed by the Probate Service.

![Probate application documents on a desk](https://img.ulec.com.cn/跨境法务-UK继承/ukinheritance-com-guide-2026-08-21-c6daa5039b-image-01-2026-1280x800.jpg)


If you ordered copies of your probate document for use outside the UK, these will take longer to arrive than your UK copy.

## Other Considerations

If your right to live in the UK depends on your relationship with someone who died, you might need to apply for a new visa. This includes cases where your partner who died served as a member of HM Forces.
