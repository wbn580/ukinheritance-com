## Before you apply for probate

Before applying for probate, you must check two things: that you are eligible to apply, and whether you need to pay Inheritance Tax.

## How your application is handled

Your application will be reviewed by the Probate Service.

![Probate application documents and Inheritance Tax forms in the UK](https://img.ukinheritance.com/跨境法务-UK继承/ukinheritance-com-guide-2026-08-10-df5001ec2a-image-01-2026-1880x1253.jpg)


If you order copies of your probate document for use outside the UK, these will take longer to arrive than your UK copy.

## Relationships and residency

If your right to live in the UK depends on your relationship with someone who died, you might need to apply for a new visa. This may apply if your partner who died served as a member of HM Forces.
