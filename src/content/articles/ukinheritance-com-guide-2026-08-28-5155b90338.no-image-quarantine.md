## Check your eligibility

Before applying for probate, you must confirm that you're eligible to apply. The Probate Service will review your application after you submit it.

## Check Inheritance Tax

You also need to find out if you need to pay Inheritance Tax before applying for probate.

## After you've applied

If you ordered copies of your probate document for use outside the UK, these will take longer to arrive than your UK copy.
